# CraftLook 0.3.0

CraftLook 0.3.0 (build 10) brings Finder thumbnails and Space-bar previews to SolidCraft, FilmCraft, MaterialCraft, EffectCraft and SoundCraft projects and to SVG files, with twice the renderers in a download of about the same size.

## Highlights

- **A new name.** ArtCraft Quick Look is now **CraftLook**, with the same kingfisher.
- **Six new formats.** SolidCraft (`.sc`, `.solidcraft`), FilmCraft (`.fcproj`), MaterialCraft (`.mcraft`), EffectCraft (`.ecproj`, `.ecprojx`) and SoundCraft (`.scraft`) projects now show what's inside, next to FigCraft, DesignCraft, VectorCraft, TextureCraft, PhotoCraft and Photoshop.
- **SVG with real text.** SVG files (`.svg`) are drawn by the VectorCraft renderer, text included.
- **Full-size thumbnails.** Finder thumbnails fill their whole frame instead of a smaller box inside it.
- **Renderers in the app only once.** The thumbnail and preview extensions share a single copy of the renderers: eight of them now fit in about the same download as the four of 0.2.5, and in about 1.6 GB once installed.
- **Parallel requests.** When Finder asks for many thumbnails at once, the renderer service queues them instead of turning them away, so a folder full of projects fills in completely.
- **FilmCraft placeholders.** A picture that Quick Look can't read, because it lives outside the project, becomes a green rounded rectangle of its size, in its place, so the frame keeps its layout.
- **SoundCraft timeline.** A session shows its tracks, clips where and as long as they play, fades, MIDI notes, markers and the playhead.
- **Latest renderers.** Every renderer is at its latest release, pre-releases included: FigCraft 0.18.1, DesignCraft 0.20.1, VectorCraft 0.24.1, TextureCraft 0.6.1, SolidCraft 0.46.2, FilmCraft 0.20.1, MaterialCraft 0.4.1 and EffectCraft 0.24.1. [Upstream releases and credits](https://github.com/Roccobot/CraftLook/blob/v0.3.0/THIRD_PARTY.md)

## Install

Download **craftlook-0.3.0-macos.zip** (about 720 MB), unzip it, move **CraftLook** into **Applications**, and open it once. The app targets **macOS 13 or later** on Intel and Apple silicon, and needs no ArtCraft app installed.

**Upgrading from 0.2.5?** The app is now called CraftLook. Quit **ArtCraft Quick Look** and delete it from **Applications** before you install CraftLook, so that the two don't handle the same files. Finder picks up the new formats by itself.

Select a supported file in Finder and press **Space**. For a small example, download [River Study](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roccobot/CraftLook/v0.3.0/examples/river-study.vectorcraft).

## Good to know

- **Not yet notarized.** The app is ad hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple, so macOS may refuse to open it the first time. Go to **System Settings**, **Privacy & Security**, and click **Open Anyway**. [Apple's guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445)
- **SVG and other previewers.** macOS uses one Quick Look extension for each file type. If Looq, Gapplin or another app previews SVG files, turn its extension off in **System Settings**, **General**, **Login Items & Extensions**, **Quick Look**, so that CraftLook shows them.
- **Files outside the project.** Quick Look doesn't let the renderer read them, so such projects may look different from the app, as the FilmCraft placeholders show.
