# CraftLook 0.4.1

CraftLook 0.4.1 (build 13) redraws the app window in the Liquid Glass style of recent macOS, with a shortcut to the Quick Look settings and the author's credit. If you have CraftLook 0.4.0, it arrives by itself: CraftLook asks before installing it, or choose **Check for Updates...** in the CraftLook menu.

## Highlights

- **A new window.** The content runs edge to edge under a transparent title bar, and the controls are grouped on Liquid Glass panels, with corners that follow the capsule buttons inside them; before macOS 26 the panels use the standard macOS material. Text and controls keep clear margins and never touch the window's edges.
- **Open System Preferences.** A new button under the update settings opens the Quick Look extensions in System Settings, with two lines on how to turn CraftLook on, or off to go back to the standard macOS previews.
- **Credits.** At the bottom of the window and in the About panel: *Made with ♥ by Rocco 'Roccobot' Casadei*, with his mark, a link to [www.roccobot.me](https://www.roccobot.me) and the GitHub and Telegram icons.

## Still in 0.4.1

- **Signed and notarized.** CraftLook is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple: Apple has checked it for malicious software, so Gatekeeper opens it without warnings.
- **Automatic updates.** Since 0.4.0, CraftLook checks for a new version once a day while it's open, and asks before installing it. Updates come through [Sparkle](https://sparkle-project.org) and are accepted only with CraftLook's EdDSA signature.
- **Why it's so large.** The download is about 720 MB, and the app about 1.6 GB once unzipped, because it carries the headless drawing engines of eight ArtCraft apps, universal for Intel and Apple silicon, to draw your projects on the fly without the apps installed. Everything else, the app itself, its extensions, Sparkle and the licenses, takes about 7 MB.

## Install

Download **craftlook-0.4.1-macos.zip** (about 720 MB), unzip it, move **CraftLook** into **Applications**, and open it once. The app targets **macOS 13 or later** on Intel and Apple silicon, and needs no ArtCraft app installed. With 0.4.0 installed, the update arrives by itself.

**Upgrading from ArtCraft Quick Look 0.2.5?** Quit it and delete it from **Applications** before you install CraftLook, so that the two don't handle the same files.
