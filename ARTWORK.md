# Kingfisher artwork

The kingfisher illustration is the visual identity of CraftLook. Its artwork, including `Resources/CraftLook.icns` and `docs/images/kingfisher.png`, is excluded from the project's MIT code license.

You may redistribute the unmodified icon as part of CraftLook, including copies built from this source, and use it to identify the app in reviews or announcements. For standalone reuse, adaptations or branding another product, please contact [Roccobot](https://github.com/Roccobot).
