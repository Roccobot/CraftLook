# Kingfisher artwork

The kingfisher illustration is the visual identity of ArtCraft Quick Look. Its artwork, including `Resources/ArtCraftQuickLook.icns` and `docs/images/kingfisher.png`, is excluded from the project's MIT code license.

You may redistribute the unmodified icon as part of ArtCraft Quick Look, including copies built from this source, and use it to identify the app in reviews or announcements. For standalone reuse, adaptations or branding another product, please contact [Roccobot](https://github.com/Roccobot).
